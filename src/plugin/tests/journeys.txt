A Briarheart and six Forsworn wear the party down: the player is injured, Lydia nearly falls, and someone dies (Lydia in a duo, Aela in a party). Brelyna heals when she is there; alone, the player drinks potions.
  [remembered event]
    A battle was fought at the road to Whiterun. It lasted 34 seconds. Kaira, Lydia, Marcurio, Aela and Brelyna fought against Forsworn Briarheart and Forsworn x6. All 7 enemies were killed. Who killed the enemies: Kaira 2 (Forsworn Briarheart and Forsworn); Lydia 2 (Forsworn x2); Marcurio 2 (Forsworn x2); Aela 1 (Forsworn). Most of the party's damage to the enemy was dealt by Kaira, Lydia and Marcurio. Lydia took the largest share of the enemy's blows. Brelyna kept the others on their feet with healing.     Aela killed 1 of the 7 who died on the other side: Forsworn.
